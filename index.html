Tower of Hanoi

Move all disks from Tower A to Tower C.

How to Play

  1. Click a tower to select it as the source.
  2. Click another tower to select it as the destination.
  3. Only the top disk can be moved, one disk per turn.
  4. A larger disk can never be placed on a smaller disk.
  5. Win by moving all disks to Tower C.
